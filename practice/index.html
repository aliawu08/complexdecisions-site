Practice

Consequential decisions, taken with the whole picture in view.

Complex Decisions works with principals facing decisions in which the variables are many, the professionals involved are several, and the sequence of the work determines the outcome.

The firm's work is the decision itself: framing the question, testing each option against what can be known, and ordering the workstreams so that each step protects the next. The professionals a principal retains carry out their disciplines; the firm holds the whole.

The firm's engagements share a recognizable shape:

  • A relationship with a counterparty that must be restructured or brought to an end on acceptable terms.
  • A timeline governed by external processes.
  • Several professional workstreams that must move in a particular order.
  • Cross-border elements, in more than one language.
  • A principal who requires a single, integrated view.

Each engagement is defined in writing: the question, the scope, the period, and the fee.
