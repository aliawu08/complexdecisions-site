Approach

Principal-led. Bounded. Quiet.

Principal-led. Every engagement is conducted personally by the firm's principal, from first meeting to close.

Bounded. An engagement is a defined question answered within a fixed period for a fixed fee. Further work is agreed separately. The fee is for the firm's commitment and availability during the period.

Quiet. Engagements are confidential, including the fact of the engagement itself. The firm's work is known only to those it is for.

Decisions stay with the principal. The firm frames, analyses, and sequences. Decisions, and the professionals who execute them, remain the principal's own.

New engagements are considered selectively and, as a rule, by introduction.
