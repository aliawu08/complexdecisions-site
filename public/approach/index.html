Principal-led. Defined. Confidential.

Principal-led. Every engagement is conducted personally by the firm's principal, from inception to conclusion.

Defined. An engagement is a defined question addressed within a fixed period for a fixed fee. Further work is agreed separately. The fee is for the firm's professional commitment and availability during the period.

Confidential. Engagements are held in strict confidence, including the existence of the engagement itself.

Authority remains with the principal. The firm frames, analyses, and sequences. Decisions, and the professionals who execute them, remain the principal's own.

New engagements are accepted selectively and by introduction.
