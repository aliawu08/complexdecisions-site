Decision consulting for consequential, high‑uncertainty matters.

Complex Decisions advises principals on decisions that involve multiple variables, several professional advisors, and a sequence of steps that determines the outcome. The firm maintains the integrated view; the professionals retained by the principal perform their respective functions.

Each engagement is defined in writing, conducted personally by the firm's principal, and held in strict confidence, including the existence of the engagement itself.
