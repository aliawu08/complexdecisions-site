Practice

The firm's work concerns the decision itself: the framing of the question, the weighing of the options, and the order in which the work proceeds. The client's advisors act within their disciplines; the firm considers the whole.

Engagements typically involve:

  • A timeline governed by external or regulatory processes.
  • Several advisors whose work must proceed in a particular order.
  • Cross-border matters conducted in more than one language.
