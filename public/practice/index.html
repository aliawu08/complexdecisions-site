Areas of engagement

The firm's work is the decision itself: defining the question, evaluating the options, and ordering the workstreams. The professionals retained by the principal perform their respective functions; the firm maintains the integrated view.

Engagements typically involve:

  • A counterparty relationship to be restructured or concluded.
  • A timeline governed by external or regulatory processes.
  • Several professional workstreams that must proceed in order.
  • Cross-border elements in more than one language.
